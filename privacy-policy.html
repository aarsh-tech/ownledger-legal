OwnLedger Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

OwnLedger is a personal money manager made by AS, an individual developer in India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the OwnLedger Android app.

The short version

  • Your financial records are stored only on your phone.
  • OwnLedger has no account, no login and no server. We never receive your records.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking in the app.
  • The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • We do not sell or share your data, because we never have it.

1. Information you keep in the app

You may enter or import wallets and account balances, income, expenses, budgets, savings goals, bills, debts, notes, and the figures you type into the tax scenario. The app also keeps a few preferences, such as whether app lock is on and when you last exported a backup.

All of this is stored in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else by OwnLedger.

2. Information we collect

None. OwnLedger does not collect or transmit personal information, financial records, contacts, location, photos, SMS messages, device identifiers or usage statistics to us.

3. Backups

Android backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy OwnLedger's database to your own Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is created and stored by Google under Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We cannot access it. You can turn it off in Android Settings.

Backup files you export. When you export a backup or a report, the file goes only where you choose (for example Google Drive, email or a computer). These files contain your financial records and are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe.

4. Statement import

When you import a bank statement, the file you pick is read on your phone. Only the transactions you confirm are saved, inside the app. The file is not uploaded anywhere.

5. App lock

If you turn on app lock, OwnLedger asks Android to confirm your fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern. Android handles this check; OwnLedger only learns whether it succeeded. Your biometric data and screen-lock credentials never reach the app or us.

6. Purchases

OwnLedger is a paid app sold through Google Play, which handles the payment. Google shares limited order information with us, such as an order number, date, country and price, as described in Google Play's terms. We never receive your card, UPI or bank details. We use order information only to provide the purchase, handle refunds and meet accounting and tax obligations.

When OwnLedger starts, it asks Android which app store installed it. Copies installed from outside Google Play do not open. This check happens only on your phone, and nothing about it is sent to us.

7. Permissions

OwnLedger does not request internet access, contacts, location, camera, microphone, SMS or storage permissions. It uses only:

  • Biometric: for the optional app lock.

Files you import or export go through Android's own file picker and share screen, which only give the app the single file you choose.

8. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information. When you use the share or export feature, the data goes to the app or place you choose.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

Your records stay on your phone until you delete them. You can delete single records, use Erase all data in Privacy & app lock, clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Copies in your Google backup are managed in your Google account, and exported files are managed by you.

10. Security

OwnLedger keeps your records in storage that only the app can access, protected by Android's built-in security, and offers an optional app lock. No method of storage is completely secure, so use a screen lock on your phone and keep exported files safe.

11. Children

OwnLedger is meant for adults managing their own money and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

12. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and similar laws you have rights over your personal data. Because we hold no personal data about you, you can see, correct and erase everything directly in the app. If you have a question or a complaint, contact us below and we will respond within 30 days.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date, and mention significant changes in the app's update notes.
